Privacy Policy — FireApp

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Zone Softech ("we", "us") built the FireApp application as a messaging app. This page explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using FireApp you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Phone number — used to create your account and verify you via a one-time SMS code (Firebase Phone Authentication).
  • Profile details — the display name, unique username, and optional profile photo you choose.
  • Messages and chats — the text messages (and any media you later choose to send) are stored so they can be delivered to the people you chat with.
  • Device / push token — a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can deliver notifications for new messages and calls.
  • Contacts (optional) — if you grant permission, your phone contacts are matched against FireApp users so you can find people you know. Matching is done to show which contacts use FireApp; we do not sell or share your contact list.

How we use the information

The information above is used only to run the core features of the app: signing you in, delivering your messages and calls, showing your profile to people you chat with, and sending notifications. We do not sell your personal data.

Third-party services

FireApp uses trusted third-party services that may process limited data to function:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging) — privacy
  • Agora — used only for voice and video calls — privacy

Data retention & deletion

Your messages remain until they are deleted. The app also offers an optional auto-delete setting (off by default) that removes messages older than a chosen number of days from your device. To delete your account and associated data, contact us at the email below and we will remove your profile and messages from our servers.

Security

Data in transit is protected using industry-standard encryption. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children's privacy

FireApp is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
